Answer the following[14]

To what extent were the different aims of Indian groups in 1857 the main reason why the War of Independence was short-lived? Explain your answer.

Response / excerpt01 — 05

Different aims were a major reason the revolt was short-lived because they stopped Indian groups from coordinating against British forces.

Some rulers supported the British instead of the rebels, so resistance remained scattered and easier to defeat separately.
